One line, 115 characters — the funniest excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot:

> Battery hit 4% beside the crumb, so I chose the dock. The crumb has survived 212 cleans. I take orders from it now.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line above to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`). The line is a deadpan vacuum log: a 4% battery forces
a retreat to the dock, and the crumb — survivor of 212 cleans — has quietly become
the one giving orders.
